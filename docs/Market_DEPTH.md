# Maekrt-depth product layer

PricingOS is deliberately not a sterile finance calculator. Real pricing decisions involve people, incentives, uncertainty and trust.

## The questions behind the model

### 1. Will customers understand the change?
A price increase can be economically rational and still fail if customers cannot explain what they are paying for. The product therefore frames AI credits as a visible value metric instead of hiding consumption behind an opaque bill.

### 2. Who gets hurt by the change?
Average revenue can improve while a small group of heavy users becomes unprofitable or churns. Segment-level economics make that risk visible before rollout.

### 3. What does the PM actually know?
The default scenario is synthetic. Every recommendation is framed as a hypothesis, with confidence and explicit assumptions. This prevents false precision.

### 4. What happens after the decision?
PricingOS ends with an experiment design, not a green light. The PM defines the primary metric and guardrails before customers are exposed to the change.

## Product principle

**Do not optimize the spreadsheet at the expense of the person.**

The strongest monetization decision is the one that improves business economics while remaining understandable, measurable and fair enough to test with real customers.

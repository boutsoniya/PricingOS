# PricingOS Metrics Framework

| Metric | Definition | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| MRR | Monthly recurring revenue | Immediate monetization outcome |
| ARR | MRR × 12 | Annualized revenue scale |
| ARPU | Revenue / active customers | Value captured per account |
| Conversion | Paying customers / eligible visitors | Demand response to price |
| Gross margin | (Revenue − variable delivery cost) / Revenue | Economic durability |
| AI cost / account | Inference and usage cost per active account | Prevents margin compression |
| Net revenue / visitor | Revenue after modeled conversion effects / visitors | Primary pricing experiment metric |
| 30-day churn | Customers lost within 30 days | Retention guardrail |

## Decision hierarchy

1. Protect unit economics.
2. Optimize customer value capture.
3. Validate demand response experimentally.
4. Scale only after guardrails remain healthy.

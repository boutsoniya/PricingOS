Decision memo
The executive call
A concise rationale a PM can take to pricing, finance and growth.

Recommendation: run a controlled price test

Decision. Move the Pro plan from $49 to $59/month and bundle 1,000 AI credits, rather than adopting uncapped usage immediately.

Why. The modeled scenario increases ARPU and gross margin while preserving a predictable customer bill. A visible usage allowance creates a natural value metric for AI-heavy accounts.

Tradeoff. The model assumes conversion falls by 1.8 percentage points. That risk is acceptable only if net revenue per visitor improves and 30-day churn stays within the guardrail.

Next step. Run a 50/50 holdout experiment, pre-register the thresholds, instrument AI cost per account, and review the cohort after 4–6 weeks.

PM call: TEST, don't ship globally

This is a monetization hypothesis—not a forecast. The experiment converts uncertainty into evidence.
